---
title: KOReader
description: Connect KOReader to Aldus for books and reading progress.
---

1. In Aldus, open **Account**.
2. Create a KOReader credential.
3. Enter the displayed server address, username, and password in KOReader.
4. Keep the credential private. It grants access to your reading data.

Delete a credential from Account when a device is lost or no longer used.
