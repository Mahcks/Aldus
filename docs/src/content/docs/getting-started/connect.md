---
title: Connect to a server
description: Connect the Aldus app to a household server.
---

You need the HTTPS address and credentials supplied by the person who runs your Aldus server.

1. Open Aldus.
2. Enter the server address, including `https://`.
3. Select **Connect**.
4. Sign in with your Aldus username and password.

Local HTTP addresses are supported for servers on your private network. Use HTTPS for servers reached over the internet.
