---
title: Explore the demo
description: Try Aldus with a temporary public-domain library.
---

1. Open [demo.aldus.media](https://demo.aldus.media).
2. Select **Explore demo**.
3. Open a title from Home or Discover.
4. Try reading, listening, and switching formats.

Demo visits expire automatically. Do not upload private media or personal information to the public demo.
