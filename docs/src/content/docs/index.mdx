## Why people switch to Aldus

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Never lose your place" icon="approve-check-circle">
    Close the ebook on your commute, open the audiobook that night, and
    resume at the same sentence — not an approximate percentage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Own it, don't rent it" icon="seti:lock">
    Your books live on your server. No account with a streaming company, no
    subscription, nothing that disappears if a company shuts down.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask for what's missing" icon="magnifier">
    Request a format you don't have. Aldus searches, downloads, verifies, and
    imports it under rules you set — optional, and entirely under your
    control.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
