_Last updated: August 25, 2026_

This page is a pre-release policy draft. Contact and retention details will be finalized before public App Store submission.

## Self-hosted Aldus servers

An Aldus server is operated by you or your server administrator. The server stores account details, library membership, media metadata, reading and listening progress, activity, and configuration needed to provide the service. The Aldus project does not receive that information merely because you use the app.

The app stores authentication credentials securely on the device and may store downloaded media, metadata, and pending progress for offline use.

## Public demo

The developer-operated demo creates a temporary guest account. It processes the guest identifier, activity, reading and listening progress, and operational network logs needed to run and protect the service. Demo media is public-domain demonstration content.

## Tracking and advertising

Aldus does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking and does not sell personal information.

## Account deletion

Self-hosted administrators control accounts on their servers. The app will provide an in-app deletion path for guest and regular accounts before public App Store release.
