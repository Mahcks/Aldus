---
title: iPhone and iPad
description: Read and listen with Aldus on iOS and iPadOS.
---

Open a title and choose **Read** or **Listen**. Aldus saves your position as you move through the book.

## Continue in another format

When a synchronized edition is available, leave the reader or player and choose the other format. Aldus resolves the saved position through the alignment instead of estimating from a percentage.

## Background listening

Audio continues when the screen locks or you switch apps. Use the system media controls to pause and resume.

## Local servers

iOS may ask for local-network access when you connect to a server in your home. Aldus uses that permission only to reach the server address you chose.
