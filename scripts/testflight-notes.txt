Aldus: reading handoff and highlights

Update your Aldus server to 0.1.0-beta.22 before testing this build. Use the same account and book on your iPhone and in the web app.

1. Move between devices
Select a passage on the web, continue on your iPhone, then move back to the web. Repeat a few times. The previous device should pause promptly, and the receiving device should open the same passage without changing its saved place.

2. Try the handoff timing
Turn a page just as you continue on the other device, and try returning to Aldus after it was in the background. Reading should pause during the transfer. A delayed page turn should not overwrite the other device's position or create an unnecessary saved-place conflict.

3. Highlight across paragraphs
Select text within one paragraph and across several paragraphs. Move between web and iPhone. The whole passage should remain highlighted, with iPhone highlights following individual text lines.

4. Save and restore
Turn pages, close the book, and reopen it. Check that the save indicator only confirms a completed save. Web reading-position labels should remain visible; page counts may differ between devices because their layouts differ.

5. Switch formats and read offline
For a matched ebook and audiobook, try Read → Listen → Read. Download an ebook, make the server unavailable, and reopen it. After reconnecting, check your saved place. If you read different places offline and on another device, both should remain available until you choose where to continue.

Please send TestFlight feedback with your iPhone model, the steps, the expected result, and what happened. Include a screenshot when useful, but hide passwords and API keys.
